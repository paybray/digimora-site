Frequently asked questions

How do I reset my level scores or settings?

There's currently no in-app reset option. Deleting and reinstalling Digimora will clear all locally stored scores and settings.

Does Digimora need an internet connection?

No. Digimora works entirely offline.

Is my progress backed up or synced between devices?

Not currently. Progress is stored only on the device you're playing on — see the privacy policy for details.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet available. If that changes, it'll be announced separately.
