Digimora Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 4, 2026

Short version: Digimora does not collect any data. There is nothing to disclose because nothing ever leaves your device.

No accounts

Digimora has no login or sign-up. The app has no way of knowing who you are.

No data collection

Digimora contains no analytics, no advertising, and no crash-reporting services, and includes no third-party SDKs of any kind.

No network access

Digimora does not send or receive data over the internet. The app works entirely offline.

Local storage only

Your settings (theme, sound preferences) and level scores are stored using Apple's standard on-device storage, directly on your iPhone or iPad. This information never leaves your device and the developer has no access to it.

Deleting the app deletes this data

Uninstalling Digimora permanently removes all locally stored settings and scores.

Children's privacy

Because Digimora does not collect data from anyone, it does not collect data from children either.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future version adds an optional online feature — this page will be updated and the date above will change accordingly.
